Best Horror Movies of all time

Top 5 Horror Movies

The Exorcists(1973)

Director: William Friedkin
-Widely regarded as one of the scariest and most influential supernatural horror films ever made.

Psycho(1960)

Director: Alfred Hitchcock
-A foundational masterclass in psychological suspense that shattered audience expectations with its mid-film pivot, iconic shower scene, and chilling portrayal of Norman Bates at the Bates Motel.

The Shining(1980)

Director: Stanley Kubrick
-Based on Stephen King's novel, this adaptation uses the isolated, snowbound Overlook Hotel to craft a suffocating, atmospheric descent into madness, driven by unforgettable imagery and a haunting performance by Jack Nicholson.

The Texas Chainsaw Massacre

Director: Tobe Hooper
-A gritty, low-budget independent milestone that redefined raw, visceral terror and helped shape the modern slasher and rural-horror subgenres.

Alien(1979)

Director: Ridley Scott
- Merging deep-space science fiction with relentless creature-feature horror, it introduced a terrifying extraterrestrial antagonist and created a tense, claustrophobic masterwork that remains unmatched.
